Verification flags used in the tables:

- **[V]** = the figure appeared in a search extract of the cited source in this session.

Conventions: Nvidia's fiscal year ends on the last Sunday of January (FY2027 ends January 2027). All amounts in US dollars. "FY2026"-style labels on SEC EDGAR link titles are SEC filing-year labels, not Nvidia fiscal years.

**Important comparability break:** starting in Q1 FY2027, Nvidia's non-GAAP measures **no longer exclude stock-based compensation** — [Nvidia Q2 FY27 press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027); [marketscreener copy of Q2 FY27 CFO commentary](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/nvidia-second-quarter-2027-cfo-commentary-ce7858d9d18df324). ### Inferences
- GAAP operating margin [D]: Q1 FY25 64.9%, Q2 FY25 62.1%, Q3 FY25 62.3%, Q4 FY25 61.1%, Q1 FY26 49.1% (H20 charge), Q2 FY26 60.8%, Q3 FY26 63.2%, Q4 FY26 65.0%, Q1 FY27 ~65.6%, Q2 FY27 ~66.2%. Operating leverage has continued: opex grew 55% y/y in Q2 FY27 while revenue grew 106%.
- In Q1 FY27 GAAP net income ($58.3B) exceeded GAAP operating income ($53.5B), and in both FY27 quarters GAAP EPS is far above non-GAAP EPS ($2.39 vs $1.87; $2.46 vs $2.22). Under the new non-GAAP definition SBC is no longer added back, so the remaining gap is essentially gains on equity investments (see section 5: cumulative unrealized gains on non-marketable securities rose from $5.3B to $9.1B in Q2, and marketable equity securities were $42.8B). GAAP net income is therefore inflated by mark-to-market gains on strategic stakes and is more volatile than operating performance.
- For charting EPS, the cleanest continuous series is GAAP diluted EPS (split-adjusted). A continuous non-GAAP series needs a recast of FY25/FY26 to the new SBC-inclusive definition; one consensus source uses $4.57 as the FY26 adjusted EPS base (vs $4.77 originally reported), which is consistent with such a recast — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/expect-nvidias-next-quarterly-earnings-151739351.html).
- Gross margin recovered from the H20-hit 60.5% (Q1 FY26) back to ~75% by Q4 FY26 and has been flat at 74.9–75.0% for three quarters; guidance now points down (74.0% in Q3 FY27, see section 4).

Data Center has grown from $22.6B (87% of revenue) in Q1 FY25 to $89.0B (93%) in Q2 FY27; all other businesses together (now reported as "Edge Computing") were $7.2B in Q2 FY27.
