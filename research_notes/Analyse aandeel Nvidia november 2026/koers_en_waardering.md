### Inferences
- The fall from the record has three separable drivers: an AI-capex and financing scare in the second half of October (-14% from 6 to 30 October, alongside the SOX), a political shock on 5 November that hit all chip stocks harder than Nvidia, and a company-specific leg from 9 November in which Nvidia fell while peers rose.
- From the 6 November close to the 11 November close Nvidia lost 6.9% while AMD gained 23%, Broadcom 17.5% and the SOX 12.5%. That gap of roughly 20–30 percentage points is a reasonable measure of the CEO-specific discount, and it suggests investors rotated into competitors.

- Even at the 12 November low the share was only back at its level of late June 2025 (last close below $150: $147.90 on 24 June 2025).

### Inferences
- The spread in "forward P/E" (11 to 25) is a definitional problem, not a data error: about 11 uses fiscal 2028 EPS (year to January 2028), about 12–15 uses the next twelve months, about 19 uses fiscal 2027, and the 21–25 figures look like current-year or outdated estimates. The report should state the earnings year whenever it quotes a forward multiple.
- On any basis Nvidia is at the bottom of its own five-year range (trailing 22 versus an average of 53–55; forward 11–15 versus 36), and below the S&P 500's forward multiple, which is unusual for a company growing revenue 65–90% a year.
- The low multiple rests on estimates that have not yet been adjusted for the CEO's departure. If analysts cut fiscal 2028 EPS by, say, 10% (to about $14.1), the fiscal 2028 multiple at $165 would be about 11.7 instead of 10.5 (own illustration, not a forecast).
- Relative to peers, Nvidia is priced at roughly a third of AMD's and about 55–60% of Broadcom's next-year multiple, while consensus revenue growth for the three is of the same order (65–90%).
- The CompaniesMarketCap figures of about $964 billion for AMD and $1.75 trillion for Broadcom are higher than the 11 November close values ($907 billion, $1.65 trillion); they probably reflect intraday 12 November prices, which would fit reports that both stocks rose that day.

### Gaps
- The files stop at the 11 November 2026 close because the market was still open when they were built; the 12 November close must be added later if the report needs it.
- stooq.com, Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq and the other finance hosts would have to be allowed in the session's network settings to download directly from them.
- The series are not dividend-adjusted; total-return charts would need the dividend amounts listed in section 5.
